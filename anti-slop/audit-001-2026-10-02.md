## Findings

1. HIGH, R-36/R-38/C-5: README claims unsupported XNOR, light/dark mode, theme persistence, dropdown selection, Canvas use, keyframe animation, and animated current flow.
2. HIGH, R-03/C-4: the circuit board uses a desktop 2:1 coordinate layout that is squeezed and clipped at narrow widths.
3. HIGH, R-03: gate-selection buttons are below the 44 x 44 px touch-target baseline.
4. HIGH, R-25: truth-table zero values use text colors below WCAG AA for 14 px text.
5. HIGH, R-25: inactive wires and bulb outlines are below the 3:1 non-text contrast threshold.
6. HIGH, R-37: no explicit design direction defines identity, palette, typography, mood, or design dials.
7. MEDIUM, R-01/R-07: cyan-blue headline gradient and technical grid background are generic techniques without documented purpose.
8. MEDIUM, R-13: glow is distributed across inputs, wires, gate, bulb, and selected buttons instead of being a focused accent.
9. MEDIUM, R-06: Inter, JetBrains Mono, and tracked uppercase labels have no documented typography rationale.
10. LOW, R-29/R-31: OFF/ON/selected/output states use fragmented red, cyan, green, yellow, and blue semantics.
11. HIGH, R-32/C-4: controls and output state lack complete accessible names and selected/status semantics.
12. LOW, R-31: JavaScript comments contain decorative separators, numbered workflow narration, and conversational implementation notes.

## Approved scope

The owner delegated prioritization and implementation on 2026-10-02. Findings 1 through 12 are approved for controlled remediation on a separate branch.
